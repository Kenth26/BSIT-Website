Bachelor of Science in Information Technology | Technology and Engineering College for Humanity

Where learning goes beyond the classroom

Being a BSIT student is more than lectures and laboratory work. Throughout the school year, the Technology and Engineering College for Humanity opens doors for students to learn from industry partners, explore new technologies and connect with fellow IT enthusiasts. From seminars and workshops to partnerships that bring emerging fields like blockchain into the curriculum, every event is a chance for students to sharpen their skills, discover their interests and prepare for their future careers in the digital world.

TECHNOVATE 2026: EMPOWERING HUMANITY THROUGH INNOVATION

For the first time, students from all programs of the Technology and Engineering College for Humanity come together for one big celebration of technology. Tech Week is the college's first major event since TECH was formed, bringing BSIT, BSCS, BSCpE and Mechanical Engineering students under one roof to compete, collaborate and showcase what they can do. From programming and cybersecurity challenges to creative tech competitions, it's a week for every DYCIan to test their skills, meet fellow innovators and prove that together, we are stronger. Learn today, create tomorrow!
