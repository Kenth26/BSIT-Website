Learn today, Create tomorrow!

The Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) program at Dr. Yanga’s Colleges prepares students to become skilled and responsible IT professionals. Through hands-on learning, students build a strong foundation in programming, web and mobile development, database management, networking, and systems analysis and design. Beyond the classroom, BSIT students apply what they learn through real projects, competitions, and industry exposure, developing the technical and problem-solving skills needed to create solutions for businesses and communities. Rooted in DYCI’s commitment to quality education, the program shapes graduates who are ready to design, develop, and innovate in the ever-growing tech industry.

History of the Program in DYCI

The Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) program at Dr. Yanga’s Colleges, Inc. (DYCI) is part of the institution’s continuing development in higher education. According to DYCI’s College Student Handbook, the College of Computer Studies received government permission to offer the BSIT program in November 2008, alongside the Bachelor of Science in Information Systems at May and Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering at December. The program was established to provide students with knowledge and practical skills in computing, software development, information systems, and other areas of information technology. Over the years, BSIT has continued to be part of DYCI’s technology-focused academic offerings, preparing students for opportunities in the growing information technology industry.

When the BSIT program first opened, a small group of students enrolled as its pioneer batch, becoming the first to take on Information Technology education at Dr. Yanga’s Colleges. Together with their instructors, they helped build the program from the ground up, adapting to a new curriculum and growing facilities. After four years of study, this first batch graduated and marked a major milestone for the College of Computer Studies, setting the foundation for the many BSIT students who followed.

Today, the BSIT program continues to welcome new students each year, each batch building on the foundation laid by those before them. Today’s students learn with modern computer laboratories, an updated curriculum, and exposure to the technologies shaping the industry today. Through seminars and competitions, they develop both technical and teamwork skills beyond the classroom. The latest batch of BSIT students carries on the program’s legacy of producing IT professionals ready for an ever-changing digital world.

Changes in Department

CCS

TECH

- For many years, the BSIT program was under the College of Computer Studies (CCS), together with Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering. In July of 2026, the college grew when the Mechanical Engineering program merged into the department. To reflect its wider range of programs, the college was renamed the Technology and Engineering College for Humanity (TECH). The change brought computing and engineering students under one college, opening more opportunities for collaboration across fields. Through it all, BSIT has remained one of the core programs of the college, continuing its mission of developing skilled and responsible IT professionals.
